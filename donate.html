Support YCBN

Youth Capacity Building Network (YCBN) is a youth-led, non-profit organisation in Uganda working with young people, communities and schools on WASH, digital skills, youth empowerment, environment and community development.

What your support helps accomplish

Support received by YCBN is used to plan and deliver activities such as:

  • WASH awareness, hygiene promotion and menstrual health education in schools and communities.
  • Digital skills and technology-awareness sessions for young people.
  • Youth empowerment activities including life skills, leadership and dialogue sessions.
  • Environmental awareness and waste management activities.
  • Volunteer coordination, safeguarding and programme administration.

Every shilling received is tracked against the programme it was raised for. We publish how funds are allocated, who our governing board is, and the reports documenting our work — so that supporters, partners and funders can hold us to account.

How funds and resources are used

YCBN is committed to transparent, accountable use of funds and resources. The table below shows the share of our expenditure directed to each area of work during our current operating period (2025 to date).

Programme Area Share of Expenditure What It Funds
WASH & Menstrual Health 35% Borehole rehabilitation, handwashing facilities, hygiene education sessions
Digital Skills & Innovation 25% AI and coding training, digital literacy sessions, innovation hub support
Youth Empowerment & Leadership 20% Leadership academies, life skills sessions, youth dialogue events
Environment & Waste Management 10% Community clean-up drives, waste segregation training, environmental awareness
Administration & Operations 10% Coordination, safeguarding, volunteer management, monitoring and evaluation

Our full organisation details, board of directors and funding sources are published on the Organization & Legal Information page. Verified engagement and programme reports are published on our Our Work page.

Our promise to supporters

  • We publish our donation instructions only once they are official and verified.
  • We publish how funds are allocated across our programme areas, and we update that breakdown as our funding mix changes.
  • On request, we provide donors with a written breakdown of how their specific contribution was used.
  • We never claim eligibility for external incentives (such as Google Ad Grants) unless confirmed by the relevant provider.
