Support Our Work

Support YCBN

Youth Capacity Building Network (YCBN) is a youth-led, non-profit organisation in Uganda working with young people, communities and schools on WASH, digital skills, youth empowerment, environment and community development.

What your support helps accomplish

Support received by YCBN is used to plan and deliver activities such as:

  • WASH awareness, hygiene promotion and menstrual health education in schools and communities.
  • Digital skills and technology-awareness sessions for young people.
  • Youth empowerment activities including life skills, leadership and dialogue sessions.
  • Environmental awareness and waste management activities.
  • Volunteer coordination, safeguarding and programme administration.

Because our projects are still being developed and funded, we do not publish cost-per-output figures at this stage. Verified reporting will be published as finances are documented and reviewed.

How funds and resources are used

YCBN is committed to transparent, accountable use of funds and resources. Financial reporting and audited statements will be published on our Organization & Legal Information page and Our Work page as they become available.
