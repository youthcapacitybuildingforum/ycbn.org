Programme Overview

The YCBN Student Career & Leadership Academy is a scalable, revenue-generating youth development programme that gives students approximately 13–21 years old the guidance, skills, relationships and opportunities they need to choose a career and lead others.

Most students finish secondary school with very little practical information about careers, no mentor, and no idea how to turn their talents into a pathway. The Academy closes that gap with structured weekly lessons, real professionals, practical challenges and a clear progression from discovering to leading.

Who it is for

Students aged approximately 13–21: secondary school learners, senior and upper-secondary students, TVET and early tertiary learners, and young people moving into work.

The student promise

“Know your career. Build your skills. Become a leader.” Every week a student gets one concrete step forward, and every level ends in something they can show — a certificate, a project or an award.

Why it is affordable to run

An 80% digital / 20% physical model means lessons, mentorship and challenges are delivered online, keeping delivery costs low and allowing YCBN to scale to hundreds of students per cohort.

The Four Programme Pillars

Everything in the Academy sits under four pillars. A student can start with one and move through all of them, and each pillar is available across the membership tiers below.

1. Career Discovery

Helping students answer the question “what am I actually good at, and what could I do with it?”

  • Career exploration across a wide range of occupations
  • Subject and course choices that match real pathways
  • Education pathways: secondary, TVET, university and gap-year options
  • Employability skills: CVs, interviews, applications and professional communication
  • Entrepreneurship and starting a business
  • Digital careers and the skills the digital economy needs

2. Leadership Academy

Turning capable students into confident, ethical young leaders who can organise and represent others.

  • Self-leadership: discipline, time management and goal setting
  • Communication and public speaking
  • Teamwork and collaboration
  • Decision-making and problem-solving
  • Conflict management and handling peer pressure
  • Ethics, integrity and student leadership

3. Mentorship Hub

Putting students in front of people who have already done the job they want to do.

  • One-to-one professional mentor sessions
  • Group mentorship with a small peer cohort
  • Open career conversations with practising professionals
  • Personal-development challenges between sessions
  • Appropriate safeguarding in all mentor interactions
  • Parental or guardian consent required for minors

4. Opportunities Hub

The part students care about most — a curated, verified feed of things they can actually apply for.

  • Scholarships, bursaries and education funding
  • Competitions, academic challenges and student awards
  • Internship and apprenticeship placements
  • Volunteering and community service
  • Youth leadership programmes and networks
  • Entrepreneurship funding and incubation opportunities

The Weekly Timetable

Every day has a purpose. The structure is deliberately light so it fits around school and homework — one focused activity a day, not a full extra timetable.

Day Session What happens
Monday Career Monday A short career lesson — one career, one pathway, one thing to think about.
Tuesday Skills Tuesday Communication, digital skills, CV writing, entrepreneurship and related practical skills.
Wednesday Leadership Challenge A practical weekly challenge — something to actually do, not just read.
Thursday Mentor Thursday A 45–60 minute virtual session with a practising professional.
Friday Opportunity Friday Scholarships, competitions, internships and youth opportunities worth applying for.
Saturday Academy Live A 60–90 minute virtual masterclass with a featured guest or mentor.
Sunday Reflection Assignment and reflection on the week, plus recognition of top participants.

Live session times and the weekly schedule are communicated to members in the Academy community each week.

The Student Journey

Every student moves through the same clear pathway, so they always know what comes next and what they are working towards.

1

Register

The student or parent completes the registration form and selects a membership tier.

2

Baseline Assessment

A Career & Leadership baseline assessment establishes where the student is starting from.

3

Level 1: Discover

Career exploration, subject and course choices, and finding the direction that fits.

4

Level 2: Develop

Building the practical skills — communication, CVs, digital skills, teamwork and entrepreneurship.

5

Level 3: Lead

Leading others — organising, speaking publicly, deciding, solving problems and handling conflict.

6

Final Practical Project

A real project that proves the student can apply what they have learned.

7

Certificate & Alumni Network

Graduating students receive their YCBN certificate and join the YCBN Alumni Network — the continuing community of former Academy students, ambassadors and young leaders.

How we handle your payment

YCBN uses an official organisational payment or merchant mechanism wherever possible.

Every payment is recorded with a reference and receipt issued to the family.

Programme fees are never collected into personal accounts.

Official payment details are always shared directly by YCBN — be cautious of anyone else asking for fees.

How the Academy Is Funded

The Academy combines earned income with sponsorship and grant support, so that it is both financially sustainable and socially inclusive.

1. Student memberships

Recurring monthly income from Academy Member and Leadership Plus students.

2. Career packages

One-off Career Direction Packages: assessment, planning session and individual guidance.

3. Holiday bootcamps

Intensive 1–2 week programmes during school holidays, priced per participant.

4. School contracts

Agreements with partner schools to deliver the Academy to a whole student body.

5. Corporate sponsorships

Companies funding cohorts, scholarships or specific programme tracks.

6. Grants

Grant support specifically for students who cannot afford membership, so access is never the barrier.

Interested in funding the Academy? Sponsorship and grant partnerships are welcome — whether you want to fund a cohort, underwrite scholarships for students who cannot pay, or partner with us as a school.
