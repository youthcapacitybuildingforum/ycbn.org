Programme Overview

The Career & Leadership Academy is a seven-week Holiday Programme from Youth Capacity Building Network (YCBN). It brings practical career guidance, university and scholarship preparation, digital skills, leadership development and one-to-one consultation together in one accessible online course.

The Academy was originally designed for learners in school. It is now open to everyone — you do not need to be enrolled anywhere, hold a qualification, or be in a particular age group to take part. Everything is delivered live online, so you can join from any part of Uganda (or beyond) with a phone, tablet or computer and an internet connection.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to make a better-informed decision about their next step: school leavers, graduates, job seekers, working professionals changing direction, parents and guardians supporting a young person, teachers, community leaders and entrepreneurs.

What you get

Twenty-one live sessions across seven weeks, eight learning areas, open consultation time with the facilitators, practical resources to keep, and a YCBN certificate at the end of the programme.

Why it is online

Google Meet removes transport costs and distance as barriers. Evening and weekend sessions are timed so participants can join around work, study, business and family commitments.

What You Will Learn

Eight learning areas run across the seven weeks. Each one combines a live session with practical exercises and time for questions.

Career Guidance & Personal Development

Understand your strengths, set realistic goals and map out a direction that fits your circumstances.

Preparing for University

What to expect, how to apply, how to plan financially and how to get the most out of higher education.

Scholarship Opportunities

Where to find funding, how to read eligibility criteria and how to put together a strong application.

Universities & Course Choices

Compare institutions and courses, and understand how subject choices connect to real career pathways.

Advanced AI & Computer Skills

Practical, up-to-date digital and AI skills you can use in study, work, business and everyday problem-solving.

Leadership Skills & Teamwork

Communication, self-leadership, collaboration, decision-making and leading with integrity.

General Consultation (Academic & Career)

Open time to put your own questions to the facilitators and work through your specific situation.

Academic Consultation

Focused guidance on study choices, subject combinations, progression routes and academic planning.

Open to Everyone

There is no enrolment requirement, qualification requirement, employer requirement or age limit. If the content is useful to you, you are welcome to register.

You might be…

  • Choosing a career or a course of study
  • Preparing to apply to university or for a scholarship
  • Looking for work, an internship or a career change
  • Running a business and wanting stronger digital and AI skills
  • Supporting a young person, a class or a community group
  • Simply wanting structured time to plan your next step

What you need

  • A phone, tablet or computer with an internet connection
  • A Google account or the ability to open a Google Meet link
  • 90 minutes on Friday, Saturday and Sunday evenings
  • A notebook or a place to keep your notes and action points
  • Willingness to ask questions and take part in discussions

Safeguarding: YCBN works with young people and follows our Safeguarding Policy. Participants under 18 should register with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian, and all online sessions are facilitated in line with that policy. Concerns can be raised through our Complaints & Feedback page.

Certificate of Completion

Certificates will be given at the end of the programme. Every participant who completes the seven-week Holiday Programme receives a YCBN Career & Leadership Academy certificate.

The certificate records the learning areas covered and can be included with a CV, a university or scholarship application, an employment application, or a professional portfolio. It is also useful evidence of continuing professional development for people already in work.

Certificate details, including how and when certificates are issued, are confirmed to registered participants during the programme. If you need written confirmation of the programme content for an employer or institution, email info@ycbn.org.

Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not answered here, contact the Academy team and we will get back to you.

Do I have to be enrolled at a school, college or university?

No. The Holiday Programme is open to everyone. There is no enrolment, qualification, employer or age requirement. The Academy was originally designed for learners in school and has since been opened up so that anyone who can benefit from career guidance, digital skills and leadership development can take part.

When does the programme run?

Seven weeks, from 13 December 2026 to 31 January 2027. Live sessions take place on Friday, Saturday and Sunday each week, from 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM East Africa Time (EAT).

Where do sessions take place?

Online, live via Google Meet. Registered participants receive the joining link and the week-by-week topic plan before the first session. A phone, tablet or computer with an internet connection is all you need.

How much does it cost, and how do I pay?

The registration fee is a one-off UGX 50,000 for the full seven weeks. Pay by Airtel Money Pay: dial *185*9#, choose Pay / Merchant Payment, enter Merchant ID 7254276, confirm the account name Youth Capacity Building Network, enter UGX 50,000 and confirm with your PIN. Keep the confirmation message as your receipt and send it to info@ycbn.org or to 0756173796 / 0760315494.

What if I cannot afford the registration fee?

Contact us before the programme starts. YCBN works with donors and partners to provide supported places so that the fee is not a barrier. See the Contact page for all the ways to reach us.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Certificates are given at the end of the programme to participants who complete the seven weeks. See the Certificate of Completion section above for details.

Can an organisation sponsor a group of participants?

Yes. YCBN welcomes sponsorships and partnerships that widen access to the Academy. Please use our Partnerships page or Support page and tell us how many places you would like to fund.

Who runs the Academy?

Youth Capacity Building Network (YCBN), a youth-led nonprofit organisation registered in Uganda (NGO Bureau Reg. No. 100355-0-26), delivering WASH, digital skills, innovation, environment and youth empowerment programmes. Read more on our About page and Transparency page.
