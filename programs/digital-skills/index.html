Digital Skills

Equipping young people with practical digital literacy and technology skills to support education, work readiness, creativity and digital participation.

Bridging the Digital Divide in Uganda

In today's rapidly changing world, access to technology and the ability to use it confidently are no longer optional鈥攖hey are fundamental to education, employment, and civic participation. However, for many young people in Uganda, the digital divide remains a significant barrier. A lack of access to computers, affordable internet, and guided training limits their ability to learn, work, and compete in the modern economy.

YCBN's Digital Skills programme is designed to change this narrative. We provide practical, hands-on learning that helps young people use computers, the internet, and digital tools safely and productively. Whether it's mastering basic software, learning to navigate the web for research, or understanding the fundamentals of online communication, we are preparing youth for a digital future.

What Our Programme Covers

  • Computer Basics: Introduction to hardware, operating systems, and essential software applications (like word processing and spreadsheets).
  • Digital Literacy: Navigating the internet safely, conducting effective online research, and using email and communication tools professionally.
  • Online Safety: Understanding digital footprints, protecting personal information, and avoiding scams and cyberbullying.
  • Work Readiness: How technology supports modern enterprise, remote work, and job searching in the digital age.

Where We Operate

We currently operate in Uganda, with a focus on pilot and community activities in the Wakiso District and surrounding areas. We work with students, out-of-school youth, and community members who are eager to acquire foundational digital skills. By targeting these communities, we aim to create a ripple effect of digital empowerment that extends to families and local businesses.

Our Impact & Evidence

We are building our digital skills activities to meet the growing demand. To date, we have successfully hosted initiatives like the webinar on "Reinventing NGOs through Technology, AI and Digital Fundraising" in May 2026, demonstrating our commitment to leveraging technology for social good.

We are committed to transparency. As we expand our digital literacy classes and open learning sessions, we will publish verified project data and impact reports on our Our Work page. We believe that digital skills are a pathway to opportunity, and we are dedicated to ensuring more young Ugandans can walk that path.
