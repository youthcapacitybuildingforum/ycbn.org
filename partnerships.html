Collaboration

Partner With YCBN

Youth Capacity Building Network works with government agencies, development partners, foundations, schools, community organisations and the private sector. We are currently developing partnerships for youth development, WASH, digital skills, environment and community development programmes.

Potential collaboration areas

WASH

Water access, sanitation facilities, hygiene and menstrual health education in schools and communities.

Digital skills & innovation

Digital literacy, coding fundamentals, AI awareness, innovation spaces and youth-led problem solving.

Environment & waste management

Environmental awareness, waste segregation, recycling, upcycling and green youth activities.

Youth empowerment

Life skills, employability, entrepreneurship mentoring and youth leadership development.

Education & schools

School-based health, hygiene and skills programmes with educational institutions.

Community development

Community mobilisation, local resource mobilisation and participatory development approaches.
