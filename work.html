October 16, 2026 • Upcoming WASH Event • Kasengejje Primary School, Wakiso

WASH for Infection Prevention & AMR Action Initiative

In conjunction with the MUG Foundation, YCBN will host the WASH for Infection Prevention & AMR Action Initiative at Kasengejje Primary School, Wakiso, delivering practical WASH skills, hygiene education, and handwashing facility support to learners and the school community.

The initiative champions a simple formula for healthier communities - Clean Water, Safe Hands, Fewer Infections, and Less AMR Risk - positioning WASH as the first line of defense against antimicrobial resistance.

October 7, 2026 • Upcoming Outreach • Namayumba, Wakiso

Unite for Girls: Empowering Communities Ahead of the International Day of the Girl Child

YCBN will join member organizations of the Wezesha Girls Network Uganda for a community outreach hosted by Raising Teenagers Uganda (RTU) from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM in Namayumba Sub-County, Wakiso District, ahead of the October 11 International Day of the Girl Child.

The coalition includes Greater Impact Africa, TAM Youth Foundation, Transcend Africa Foundation, Health Promotion & Rights Watch - Uganda, Extend A Life Initiative Uganda, Brave Movement, Dream Teens Uganda, Glow Up Pads, TWEFU, Youth Chaperon Uganda, and Amity for Humanity Initiative - Uganda.

The initiative will distribute scholastic materials, provide sanitary pads and menstrual hygiene education, and deliver mentorship and sensitization sessions under the rallying cry, "When girls unite, communities transform."

August 30, 2026 • Continental Dialogue • Google Meet • Completed

Who Pays for WASH When Crisis Becomes Prolonged?

This Continental Dialogue brought together WASH practitioners and young changemakers to rethink global humanitarian financing for WASH resilience and recovery when crises become prolonged. Hosted by Frank Mwanje (Executive Director, YCBN) with guest speaker Eng. Salam Mohammad (WASH Expert & Humanitarian Consultant, Palestine) and Marwa Jamal (WASH Officer, Sudan), the session explored how WASH resilience and recovery can be financed more equitably. A Certificate of Appreciation was presented to Eng. Salam Mohammad in recognition of his invaluable insights, expertise and contribution to the dialogue.
