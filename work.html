October 10, 2026 • Appreciation • Global WASH Advocacy

Grateful for Your Inspiring Leadership in WASH Advocacy

YCBN extends its sincere appreciation to Mr. Dominic O'Neill, Executive Director of the UN's Sanitation and Hygiene Fund, for sharing his valuable insights with African and global WASH advocates. Your emphasis on advocacy and collective responsibility reinforces the need for united action to achieve safely managed sanitation for all.

Thank you for inspiring young people to be part of the solution!

October 10, 2026 • Global Dialogue • Google Meet

YCBN Online Global Dialogue on WASH: Global WASH Accountability

YCBN is bringing together African and global WASH practitioners, aspiring WASH ambassadors, development partners, donors, government institutions, and youth leaders to discuss accountability in water, sanitation and hygiene.

Guest speaker Dominic O'Neill, Executive Director of The Sanitation and Hygiene Fund (SHF), will join host Mwanje Frank and moderator Simon Peter Kigozi for a timely conversation.

October 16, 2026 • Upcoming WASH Event • Kasengejje Primary School, Wakiso

WASH for Infection Prevention & AMR Action Initiative

In conjunction with the MUG Foundation, YCBN will host the WASH for Infection Prevention & AMR Action Initiative at Kasengejje Primary School in Wakiso.

The initiative champions a simple formula for healthier communities - Clean Water, Safe Hands, Fewer Infections, and Less AMR Risk.
