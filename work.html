October 16, 2026 • WASH Donation • Kasengejje

YCBN Donates Hand Washing Facility to Kasengejje Primary School

As the world marked Global Handwashing Day, YCBN was proud to donate a hand washing facility to the pupils of Kasengejje Primary School. A simple facility but a powerful step towards better hygiene, healthier children and a brighter future.

We believe that small actions can create lasting change and together we can make hygiene a daily habit for every child!
