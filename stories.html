August 9, 2026 Continental Dialogue

How Small NGOs Can Compete for Large Funding Opportunities

YCBN convened a continental online dialogue equipping emerging organizations with practical strategies for winning major grants. The session was hosted and moderated by Mr. Simon Peter Kigozi and featured keynote speaker Ms. Margaret Gitau, Founder and Lead Consultant at NobleCause Consultancy Firm in Nairobi, Kenya.

The discussion explored five pillars of competitive positioning: governance, financial systems, impact evidence, digital credibility, and funding diversification, alongside the Pain-Gap-Stakes model and a 90-day action plan.
