Who We Are

Youth Capacity Building Network (YCBN) is a youth-led development organisation working with young people, communities and schools in Uganda.

We combine sustainable WASH services with digital skills, innovation and entrepreneurship support to build resilient communities and create lasting opportunity for young people.

Learn More About YCBN Explore Our Programmes

Safe Water Access

Drilling and rehabilitating boreholes and community wells for clean water.

Solid Waste Management

We empower communities to manage waste sustainably, promoting a cleaner environment and healthier living.

Digital Skills

AI fundamentals, coding, agritech, and fintech training for youth.

Innovation Hubs

Community innovation spaces and startup incubation support.

Global WASH Dialogue
November 8, 2026 • Water Justice

YCBN Global WASH Dialogue: Beyond Water Access, Towards Water Justice for All

YCBN convened government, diplomatic and civil-society voices online to ask what governments must do to guarantee water justice for all. Hosted by Executive Director Frank Mwanje with Mr. Godfrey Madyauta and H.E. Mags Gaynor.

WASH Donation
October 16, 2026 • WASH Programme

YCBN Donates Hand Washing Facility to Kasengejje Primary School

As the world marked Global Handwashing Day, YCBN donated a hand washing facility to the pupils of Kasengejje Primary School - a simple facility but a powerful step towards better hygiene, healthier children and a brighter future.
