Programme Area Share of Expenditure Primary Outputs

Funding Sources

  • Individual donations from Ugandan and diaspora supporters, received by bank transfer to our Centenary Bank account or by Airtel Money Pay to our registered merchant ID 7254276.
  • Partnerships and in-kind support from organisations engaging with us on WASH, menstrual health and youth skills activities.
  • Volunteer contributions of time and professional expertise, which are not financially compensated.

Audited statements and annual accounts

YCBN's annual accounts are independently reviewed before each financial year closes and are published on this page and on our Our Work page. If you require a signed financial statement, audited accounts or a funding proposal, email info@ycbn.org and we will provide it within 5 working days.

Every donation received is acknowledged in writing on request, and donors are welcome to ask for a breakdown of how their specific contribution was used.

Our Accountability Commitments

  • Non-commercial mission. YCBN exists to deliver youth development, WASH and community resilience programmes. We do not sell products or services as our primary activity, and any commercial activity is undertaken only where it directly supports our charitable purpose.
  • Published impact. Beneficiaries reached, activities delivered and partner engagements are published with dates on our Our Work and News pages.
  • Safeguarding. Our child and youth safeguarding commitments are published on the Safeguarding page, including how concerns are handled.
  • Complaints and feedback. Anyone affected by our work can raise a concern through our published Complaints & Feedback process. We do not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
  • Privacy and data protection. How we collect, use and protect personal data is set out in our Privacy Policy, and the terms of use for this website are in our Terms of Use.
  • Responsible advertising. We do not run intrusive advertising on this website, and any advertising we do carry will be relevant to our mission and limited in quantity.
